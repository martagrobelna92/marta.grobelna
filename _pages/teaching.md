Teaching has been a large part of my PhD. I have coordinated a team of 20 tutors for a course with around 800 students, run research seminars since 2024, and supervised Bachelor's and Master's theses on stochastic games and on [Automata Tutor]({{ '/projects/automata_tutor/' | relative_url }}).

## Courses

**Discrete Structures**, head teaching assistant and tutor<br>
_Winter 2025/26 · Mandatory mathematics course with around 800 Computer Science students_

- Organized the exercise sessions and coordinated a team of 20 student tutors.
- Prepared the exercise sheets and ran the course infrastructure on Moodle and TUMexam.
- Taught three exercise groups myself.

**Seminar: Model Checking Stochastic Games**, instructor<br>
_Winter 2024/25, Summer 2025, Winter 2025/26 · Advanced research seminar_

- Supervise six students per semester as they study and present research papers on the formal verification of stochastic games.

**Seminar: Recent Advances in the Verification of Neural Networks**, instructor<br>
_Winter 2024/25 · Advanced research seminar_

- Supervised students as they studied and presented recent research on verifying neural networks.

**Introduction to Theoretical Computer Science**, tutor<br>
_Summer 2024 · Mandatory course on automata theory and formal languages_

- Taught an exercise group of 15 students.
